💚 About OUTSIDE

Social media was supposed to bring us closer together. But sometimes, it feels like we're more disconnected than ever.

OUTSIDE was created with a simple mission:

Use technology to strengthen real-world friendships, not replace them.

No endless feeds. No popularity contests. Just genuine connections and memorable experiences.

✨ Features

- 🫂 Private Friend Circles — Connect with people you know.
- 📱 QR Code Invitations — Add friends by scanning a QR code.
- 🔗 Invitation Links — Share private invitations with friends.
- 🔒 Friend Requests — Approve connections before adding them.
- 📅 Hangout Planner — Organize activities with friends.
- 🌳 Activity Inspiration — Discover ideas for spending time together.
- ⏳ Focus Timer — Encourage screen-free moments.
- 🔔 Notifications — Keep up with invitations and plans.
- 🛡️ Privacy Controls — Manage your connections and account settings.

🔐 Privacy First

Your friendships should belong to you.

OUTSIDE is designed around these principles:

- No public location tracking.
- No endless social media feed.
- Private, approval-based friend connections.
- User-controlled invitations.
- Privacy-focused account settings.

Privacy and security features are still being tested during beta. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information.
